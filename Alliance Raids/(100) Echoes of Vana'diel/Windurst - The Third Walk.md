# Windurst: The Third Walk

##### Shantotto the Demon

- Be behind boss when she faces you
- When stone walls come, be center between two thinnest segments
  - Later, there is a knockback BEFORE walls collapse. Use the wall to stop yourself
- When boss traces a route, follow it to each node with the boss, where she will cast donut AoEs
  - Get out after the last one for a circle AoE

##### Alexander Resurrected

- DIVINE ARROW - Be under boss, then get behind and rotate to avoid fire
- IMPARTIAL RULING - Be middle, on the side of the boss with the slower filling meter. After half-room cleave, move to the other half
- Be out of the shapes blue lines on the floor trace. They blow up
- Kill adds that don't have shields up
  - Shields reflect
- Adds later blow up in the order they are charged by the boss
  - This happens when they are untargetable

##### Promathia

- Purple balls explode when they reach the end of their route
- WHEEL OF IMPREGNABILITY - be out of orange ring
  - Crash boss for donut after this - he moves for good measure
- Add Phase: Put your back to a wall. You can be fall off and be knocked off by knockback
- Purple balls again, go to the side where balls start closest together
- During starburst, watch boss's glowy hand for a half-room cleave
- Quickly leave stack towers after they hit, you'll be grav'ed and they blow up

##### Shinryu Paradox

- COSMIC BREATH - Jump in the hole
- COSMIC TAIL - Use teleportal to bounce back up
- CLOAK OF TWILIGHT - Be on platform that matches your color
  - Yellow up, purple down
- Countdown above your head has one of three options:
  - Eye: Look away from boss
  - Eye with ?: Look at boss
  - Pause: Stop moving
  - Play: Be moving
- RIGHT/LEFT SWORDCROSS - Be in the far opposite front corner relative to the boss
  - Or, if facing boss - RIGHT go front right, LEFT go front-left
- TWIN BLAZE - Be on the "donut" side when a purple dot and ring appear in front of the boss
