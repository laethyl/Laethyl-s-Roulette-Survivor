# San d'Oria: The Second Walk

##### Faithbound Kirin

- SYNCHRONIZED STRIKE - Go in after column AoE, former safe zones will be hit
  - After 2nd strike, stay out of flanks, arms do their strike but their's isn't rotated
- WRINGER - Crash Boss after circle for donut
- Be in front half of boss when tail glows (CRIMSON RIDDLE)
- Be behind the front half of boss if mouth glows with fire (CRIMSON RIDDLE)
- Adds:
  - Byakko - Be out of center and away from the end of his dash
    - At the end of the dash attack there is a large circle AoE
  - Seiryu - Rotational AoE
  - Suzaku - Be far out of his line for a dash. Be FAR from fire orbs telegraph, they expand
  - Genbu - Be far from water spouts
    - Donut after he hops
- STRIKING LEFT/RIGHT - Be away from targetted floating arm
  - SMITING RIGHT/LEFT SEQUENCE - Boss will telegraph the next move
- DOUBLE WRINGER - Be at boss's flank AFTER circle AoE, just outside of its hitbox
  - Then crash boss's front or back

##### Ultima, the Feared and Omega, the One

- You can fall off.
- Watch Ultima's side for circles that fire column AoE lasers
  - Screens will later copy the lasers in the new direction, NOT reflect them
- Watch for liferaft during the suck-in, it will do a half-room AoE

##### Kam'lanaut

- ELEMENTAL BLADE - Two colored rings appear on boss, REMEMBER THESE
  - Later there are three
- SUBLIME ELEMENTS - The two colors from before will expand
- When swords come, be behind their hilts
- GREAT WHEEL - Be behind boss after circle AoE
  - He rotates during the circle AoE
- TRANSCENDENT UNION - Brutal raid-wide
- Expanding color rings trigger same color crystals for large circle AoE
  - Color rings themselves do no damage
- Clones will do line AoEs, crash boss for knockback after. Aim to be launched down a walkway, or die.

##### Eald'narche

- Watch cards around boss. Sphere = circle AoE, Cylinder = donut
- Be on the opposite side when all the cards are on one side
- WARP - crash the red ground circle that's away from the boss
  - When boss arrives, be BEHIND him
- VISIONS OF PARADISE - Tethered cross will move to the indicated location, then go off
  - Only one square will be safe
