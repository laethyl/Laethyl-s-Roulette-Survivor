# AAC Heavyweight: M2

##### The Xtremes 

- Red Hot
  - Drop orange symbols and the cone away from others, dodge fire.

- Deep Blue
  - SICK SWELL - Crash side blue streaks move toward for a knockback
    - Watch for boss in the wave, be out of that column or he will hit you
  - DEEP VARIAL - Crash a corner in the direction the arrow is pointing
    - Boss does a cone from the wave

- Red Hot and Deep Blue
  - When marked, a cone will come at you matching your color to the origin symbol. Aim away from others
  - SICK SWELL can still happen, don't get knocked into fire
  - Be away from orbs that appear above fire
