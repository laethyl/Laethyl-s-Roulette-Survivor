# AAC Light-heavyweight: M4

##### Wicked Thunder

- SIDEWISE SPARK - Be on opposit side of the arena from glowy hand
- SHADOWS' SABBATH - clone spawns that uses SIDEWISE SPARK
  - Boss loves to spam the shit out of both of these, always be watching for it from boss and clones
- STAMPEDING THUNDER - When boss leaves the arena, be far away from where the huge ass gun is shooting
  - That whole 3/4ths of the arena will eventually drop away
- THREE/FOUR/FIVE-FOLD BLAST - Be dead center on the boss and memorize the pattern for the front/back attacks
- BEWITCHING FLIGHT - Be between the purple lasers
  - They carve lines that eventually get wider and blow up
  - Later, a clone will also do this at a right angle from the boss
