# AAC Heavyweight: M3

##### The Lindwurm

- ANY time a dragon head-arm appears, there will be a half-room cleave on that side
- When dragon arm reaches across the arena, follow it and be behind its head when it turns around
- Be well away from red-orange goo puddles, they do a large circle AoE explosion
- CRUEL COIL - Escape out the gap in the coil. When boss rotates, the gap will rotate 90 degrees to the left
- GROTESQUERIE - Be away from other players when YELLOW debuff expires
- BRING DOWN THE HOUSE - Don't be under tentacles
- Don't be in front of dragon heads that appear off the edges
- GROTESQUERIE - knockback when GREEN debuff expires in the direction of the arrow underneath you
  - Look away from the group when the PURPLE debuff expires, a cone AoE fires out of you

- Final Phase - Be well to the other side of a dragon head-arm surrounded by grean poison
  - It does a 2/3+ arena cleave
