# AAC Cruiserweight: M3

##### Brute Abombinator

- STONERINGER: THE STABBING/BLUDGEONING - Boss gets a giant sword or club
- BRUTISH SWING - Sword = in, Club = out
  - Sword is a donut, Club is a large circle AoE centered on the boss
  - When boss later has both weapons, the glowing one will execute the attack
- QUARRY SWAMP - Be behind adds (living or dead) or be petrified and die
  - This also kills the adds
- NEO-BOMBARIAN SPECIAL - Be at the North-center edge of the map or die
- When boss does a vine-tether, he will move to the other end, then do BRUTISH SWING
  - Donut is super tight to the edge!
- LASHING LARIOT - Watch boss for glowing hand, 2/3rd room cleave on that side
