# AAC Light-heavyweight: M3

##### Brute Bomber

- BRUTAL LARIAT - Be far on the opposite side of glowing fist
  - Covers something like 2/3rds the arena
- MURDEROUS MIST - Be DIRECTLY behind the boss for a 270 degree wide AoE
- FUSES OF FURY - Be away from bombs with "short" fuses, they will blow first, followed by long-fuse bombs
- DOPING DRAUGHT - Adds another attack to BRUTAL LARIOT, just dodge flaming fist again
  - Boss will then of course combo this with all of the above. BRUTAL LARIOT always goes 2nd!
