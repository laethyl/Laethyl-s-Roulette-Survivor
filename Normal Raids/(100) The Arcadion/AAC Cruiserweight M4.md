# AAC Cruiserweight: M4

##### Howling Blade

- WOLVES' REIGN - Each cast does the next one in line:
  - Be away from boss and between wolves - boss does circles AoE on himself, and wolves do one each at the edges
  - Don't let boss face you for next cast, a dash across the arena
  - Crash boss's dash-end point for final cast, a huge cone
- BECKON MOONLIGHT - Observe order clones appear
  - Watch their blades, each does a half-room cleave in that direction in the order they appeared
- SHADOWCHASE - Be directly behind any add
- BARE FANGS - Glowing "fangs" shoot lasers in the order they appear
- TERRESTRIAL TITANS - Pillars will fall across the arena
