# AAC Heavyweight: M3

##### The Tyrant

- Boss changes weapons:
  - Scythe: Tiny Donut
  - Axe: Circle AoE
  - Sword: Cross AoE
- SMASHDOWN - Boss uses his weapon to do the above attack
- VOID STARDUST - Purple marker, drop repeated circles at the edge
- ASSAULT EVOLVED - Boss dashes to weapons on the ground and uses their attacks.
  - Be near scythe
- Be far from large white lines cut into the floor
- When rocks come, do not let stackup marker touch them
- DOUBLE TYRANNHILATION - Be behind TWO rocks

- After arena split:
  - Watch edges for portals
  - Knockback towers - position to be knocked to the other platform or lengthwise on your platform
  - ARCADION AVALANCHE - Use knockback towers to get behind boss
    - Be center after this, boss will grab the other platform and throw it at you. Dodge accordingly
