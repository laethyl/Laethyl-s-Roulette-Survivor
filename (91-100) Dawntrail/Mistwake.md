# Mistwake

##### Treno Catoblepas

- Thunder II - Avoid rocks, players, other purple circles
  - This can break rocks
- BEDEVILING LIGHT - Get behind a rocks
- RAY OF LIGHT - Don't aim line stackup at rocks

##### Amdusias

- Don't be in front of any glowing orbs
- BIO II - balls explode not long after boss charges through them
  - Run to the first ones to explode after choosing a safe initial spot
- Thunder IV - Be away from remaining green orbs, this explodes them

##### The Thundergust Griffin

- THUNGERING ROAR - Look for safe spot from lines coming from each orb
  - They put out column AoEs
  - Eventual tornado on the edge puts out another one
