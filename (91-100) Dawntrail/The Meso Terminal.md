# The Meso Terminal

##### Chirurgeon General

- Use blue knockback to end up behind the far small circle AoE
- BIOCHEMICAL FRONT/BACK - Behind boss for FRONT, in front for BACK
- Ignore all new markers that appear after SENSORY DEPRIVATION
  - This can be another knockback, more circle AoEs, etc

##### Hooded Headsmen

- You can only attack the boss with your same debuff
- Blades cross your field in the order they appear
- Hug the edge between spiky balls
- DPS: Kill add
- Tank: Watch for interrupt
- Healer: Cleanse doom

##### The Immortal Remains

- Be away from groups of glowy NPCs
  - Aim to end up away from them when the knockback pool comes
- Watch hands for typical half-arena smash
- Be away from running glowy NPCs, large column AoE will follow in their steps
  - One group runs diagonally, dodge to the back-quarter of the arena away from them
