# The Underkeep

##### Gargant

- ALMIGHTY RACKET - Be behind boss when it faces you
- Don't be in front off boss when it leaves arena
  - It will dash out from behind a sandfall
- Little brown orbs explode in the order they appear
  - Watch the boss, it leaves the arena and does its dash during this

##### Soldier S0

- SECTOR BISECTOR - Half-room AoE from the last clone standing
- ORDERED FIRE - Columns AoEs from purple circles at the edge of the arena

##### Valie Pira

- Pink balls explode cross AoEs when purple cubes touch them
  - Crosses are relative to the edges
- Watch for column AoEs from turrets at the edges
  - No castbar, they light up with an on-screen dialogue warning
- NEUTRALIZE FRONT LINES - Be behind boss
